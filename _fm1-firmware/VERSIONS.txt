Slimehedron for the FM-1: which build is which. slimehedron-fm1.fwsc in this folder is always the newest; the git
history keeps every older one (git log -- _fm1-firmware/slimehedron-fm1.fwsc). Check a file: its sha256 below.

2026-11-18  MIDI, your own chords and drums, playable microtones, faster still
  sha256 ad2efc2f55d0b548fb3b283806ccffcc76a08ce8a320557e90b9bc37bfff2a93
  - a key used to play twice (SLOOP's own keyboard was still running underneath: C# in C major sounded C# and C);
    now each key plays its one note, in the scale
  - what you play steers the band's chord, as on the site; CHORDS (EDIT x2): up to 8 chords, the site's 20 famous
    progressions, a key sets a chord; DRUMS (GLO x2): your own 16-step pattern on the white keys
  - MIDI: in on the jack and USB (notes, drums on 10, CCs, clock, start / stop), out on USB (every part, the clock;
    microtonal notes with their own bends)
  - 19- and 31-note scales: each key is the next step (measured in the emulator: 38.5 and 38.8 cents a key)
  - fm1-emu strict: default ~52 fps (any 5 s), a cube with portals on the FX page ~42, a whole session with every
    control ~32 at worst, everything maxed with the FM sound set ~15 (the FM voices take half the chip); 0 late notes

2026-11-18  smooth and steady: 3x the frame rate, no stuck notes, portals that stay, a splash, waits for PLAY
  sha256 b88f2108b8d53b44edb0f6714dd49071a4217150774bf23cddfd62f69b1649e5
  - the goo is a hundred bigger drops (the same amount of slime, filling as fast): far less work for the chip
  - drawing is cheaper (integer colour blends, only what each row needs)
  - no note sticks to a wall: a note rattling in a corner floats back to the middle, a turning shape pushes notes back
    in instead of letting them through, and the melody keeps about 8 notes a bar in any shape (the site's rule)
  - portals rest two bars on their walls before they move
  - the fx say delay and reverb (delay time, reverb size, dub delay); the home page's ALGORITHM says portals on / off
  - power-on: the slime guy drops in with a jingle, then the tank waits for PLAY (it blinks); keys and knobs work
  - fm1-emu strict, a cube with portals and spin for 3 min: ~50 fps (was ~15), never under 40 fps over any 5 s, the
    slowest frame 62 ms (was 667 ms), 0 late notes

2026-11-18  every button does something: loop, songs, notes, double taps; the site's drum bus
  sha256 4e09c2384d17342ba9f95d298359001cda5a6d2af77f1d441d9802bb22ef6a41
  - REC records a loop of what you play (count-in on the beat, closes at the bar, overdub, x2 clears); it moves
    with the key and the scale
  - SAVE keeps 3 songs (sounds, fx, mix, notes, the loop...); the session saves itself when stopped and comes back
    at power-on; ENV is a notes page (attack, decay, sustain, release, glide, 5 note shapes)
  - double taps: PLAY from the top, LFO pins the key, SEQ drums alone, SCL the scale before; OCT- + OCT+ middle
  - the drums go through the site's drum bus (glue, body, presence dip), each kit's glue within 1 dB of the site's
  - fm1-emu strict, a whole 2-minute session with every control: 0 late notes, audio 66% of its deadline at worst,
    ~22 fps; the session came back after a power cycle (loop and all)
