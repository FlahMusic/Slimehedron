# 0014 Lessons: four merges, 3 or 5 rounds, minor and modes last, nothing cut

Status: accepted 2026-11-05 (Flah). Spec: `_docs/specs/2026-11-03-flah-critique/` (`units-audit.md`, `logic-pass.md`).

- Flah (2026-11-05): "2 is fine for loop, merge the lessons together that you find make sense, also dont make it where
  u have to do the same shit 10 times ... make it like 3 or 5 times max depending on how long or involved each thing
  is ... if you have actual proof verified by research studies that kids cant hear minor and modes outside of major
  ... prove me wrong. otherwise keep it in just make them as the final lessons".
- Make a Loop stays lesson 2 (its comment: the retention data for that slot).
- Merged, as part 2 of the lesson they belong to (a part runs straight after, the lesson ends once, a part is
  never on the map alone): Up or Down after High and Low; Find Home after The Home Note; Two at Once (intervals)
  after Three at Once (chords); Read a Rhythm (a real tune) after Count to Four. 29 lessons -> 27.
- Rounds: 5 for a quick question (one sound, one answer: the ear lessons, Steady Beat, How Long, Copy the Beat),
  3 where a round is a whole bar or phrase (the read units, Read a Tune, Echo) and for a part 2. Lessons that grow
  (Five Notes, Seven Notes, Copy the Beat) step up sooner so the five rounds still reach the hard part.
- Minor and modes (Seven Dark Notes, Raise a Note, More Scales, Your Turn: Minor) are kept and moved to the end.
  The research found no study showing children cannot hear them. What exists is about LABELLING mode as happy or
  sad, not hearing it: Dalla Bella, Peretz, Rousseau & Gosselin 2001 (Cognition 80, B1-B10): 6-8-year-olds use
  mode like adults, 5-year-olds use tempo only, 3-4-year-olds neither; Gerardi & Gerken 1995 (Music Perception
  12:3): mode moved valence judgements from age 8; Kastner & Crowder 1990 (Music Perception 8:2): from age 3.
  No study found on children telling Dorian from Mixolydian, either way. So the evidence says the happy/sad
  association comes later, not that the difference is inaudible: Flah's call stands.
- Rules out: cutting minorshapes or modes on a hunch. Moving them earlier again, or a new merge, needs a new ADR.
