# 0005 In play, the first sound and the notes belong to the child

Status: accepted (first sound 2026-10-09; auto-notes 2026-10-27)

- Play opens silent. A touch rings the nearest wall at once (no quantize on a hand, ~60 ms) and the band comes in
  400 ms after the first touch (or after 9 s untouched, as a rescue).
- The slime switch moves the key, the spin and the shapes. Dropping notes by itself is a separate switch
  (auto-notes), OFF in play and ON in studio.
- New interaction in play is a gesture on the tank before it is a button (spin, hold to loop, strum).

Why: a child who taps while a band is already playing cannot hear that they caused anything, and Flah found play
"too automated: pick the sound and the genre and it just plays".
