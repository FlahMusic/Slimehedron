# Slimehedron — Midjourney Prompts (Crayon Style, Group Scenes)

Themed art of a whole **gang of pastel crayon slimes** — one scene per learn-mode, matched to the song vibe
set for each mode. The seafoam glasses slime is just ONE of the group, not the center of attention.

---

## STEP 1 — lock the crayon look with an sref

`--sref` (style reference) makes every image share the hand-drawn crayon look of your base slime.

Your base slime URL (already filled into every prompt below):
`https://media.discordapp.net/attachments/991688319769530389/1532611921591664651/flahobino_poorly_drawn_with_mistakes_hand_drawn_kids_crayola_cr_62fa528c-2540-445d-92d6-3e6c9f74e582.png`

- `--sref <url>` — the **style** (crayon look). Carries the whole aesthetic. This is the important one.
- `--oref <url>` — optional character lock (v7; on v8 it silently renders that job on v7). Since these are
  GROUP scenes with lots of varied buddies, you often DON'T want a hard character lock — it fights the variety.
  It's kept in the prompts at a low `--ow 50` so the seafoam-glasses slime stays on-model without forcing the
  others to copy him. **Drop `--oref … --ow 50` entirely if you want maximum buddy variety / to stay pure v8.**

> ⚠️ Discord links EXPIRE (the `?ex=…` part). If a prompt errors on the URL, re-upload the slime, grab the
> fresh link, and find-replace it here.

## The cast (describe the GROUP, not one hero)

> a friendly group of gumdrop-shaped crayon slime kids in assorted pastel colors — seafoam green, lavender,
> peach, pink, pale yellow, baby blue — each a slightly different squishy shape and size, with rosy cheeks and
> simple happy faces. **Only ONE slime (seafoam green) wears round glasses**; the others have no glasses and
> varied expressions (^_^, o_o, ^o^, closed-smile) so the crowd feels varied, not cloned.

**Reusable style tail (already appended to each prompt):**
```
poorly drawn with mistakes, hand-drawn kids crayola crayon drawing style, thick waxy crayon strokes, visible
paper texture, wobbly childlike lines, pastel palette, wholesome and cute, no text --ar 16:9 --sref <URL> --oref <URL> --ow 50
```

## Extras

**Want the glasses slime as the clear hero for the logo/app instead?** use the solo prompt:
`a single gumdrop-shaped seafoam-green crayon slime kid with round glasses, rosy cheeks, ^_^ face, head and body only, centered, blank white background for easy crop` + the style tail, `--ar 1:1`.

**In-app scene backgrounds** (art behind the note geometry): add
`flat simple background, lots of empty calm sky in the center for a UI overlay` and use `--ar 3:2`.

**Max variety / pure v8:** delete `--oref … --ow 50` from any prompt — you'll lose the soft character lock on
the glasses slime but the buddy crowd gets more varied and it runs natively on the newest model.

**Consistency note:** these are GROUP scenes, so don't crank `--ow` high — a high omni weight makes every slime
try to look like the one reference (clones). Keep it ≤50, or drop it, to preserve the varied crowd.
