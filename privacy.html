Privacy

Slimehedron is a free music toy for children that runs in a browser tab.

It collects nothing. There is no account, no tracking and no server we control. Everything a child makes stays in the browser on the device they made it on.

Open Slimehedron

The questions, answered

Do you collect personal information?
No. Not a name, not an email, not an age. The app never asks for any.
Do children need an account?
No. There is no account system at all. No login, no sign-up, no password, nothing to reset.
Do you use cookies?
No. The app sets no cookies of any kind.
Do you use analytics?
No. No Google Analytics, no Plausible, no pixel, no beacon, no error reporting service.
Are there third parties?
No. The page loads no fonts, scripts, ads or images from anyone else's server. Every file it uses comes from the same address as the page itself.
Where do recordings go?
Into the browser's own storage on that device, in a database called slimehedron-rec. They are never uploaded. There is nowhere for them to be uploaded to.
What about settings and lesson progress?
Same thing. They sit in the browser's localStorage on that device, under keys starting slimehedron-. Progress is per-device and per-browser.
What happens if you clear your browser?
The recordings and the progress are gone. Permanently. There is no copy anywhere else and no login that can bring them back. That is the trade for not having accounts, and on a shared classroom machine it is a feature.
Does the microphone get used?
No. The app never asks for microphone access. Recording captures the sound the app is making itself, inside the browser. Nothing is listening to the room.
Camera? Location?
No, and no. Neither is requested or used.
What about MIDI?
Studio mode can talk to a MIDI keyboard if you have one. Your browser asks you first, and you can say no — the on-screen keyboard works either way. It is only used to send and receive notes. It is off unless you turn it on.
Does anything ever leave the device?
Only if a person deliberately makes it. There is a share button that hands a recording, or a link to the app, to your own device's share sheet. Nothing happens until you tap it and pick where it goes, and it goes wherever you picked — not to us.
Does the site keep server logs?
Yes, and it should be said plainly. See below.
Who do I contact?
flahobino@gmail.com

The one thing that is logged

Slimehedron is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like every web host on earth, GitHub keeps standard server logs when it serves the page: IP address, time, which file was requested, browser type. That happens at the hosting layer, before any of our code runs, and it is the same thing that happens when you load any website.

We do not see those logs, we do not have an account feature that could connect them to a child, and the app itself sends nothing back. But the logs exist, and a privacy page that did not mention them would be lying by omission. GitHub's handling of them is covered by the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If your district needs even that removed: the app is one folder of static files under the GPL. Host it on your own server or your own intranet and the logs are yours.

Check this yourself

You do not have to take our word for any of it. Three ways to prove it in under a minute.

1. Watch the network.

  1. Open Slimehedron.
  2. Press F12 (or Cmd+Option+I on a Mac) and click the Network tab.
  3. Reload the page. Play with it. Record something.
  4. Look at the Domain column. Every request goes to the one domain the page is on. There is no other domain in the list.

2. Pull the plug.

  1. Load Slimehedron once.
  2. Turn off your wifi, or switch the device to aeroplane mode.
  3. Reload. It still works — lessons, sound, recording, all of it.

An app that keeps running with the network off is not sending anything anywhere.

3. Read the code.

All of it is public, under the GPL-3.0-or-later, at github.com/FlahMusic/Slimehedron. Search it for fetch, XMLHttpRequest or sendBeacon — the three ways a web page sends data out. There are no matches. Search it for getUserMedia, the only way a page can reach the microphone or camera. No matches there either.

If you find something that contradicts this page, email it to us and we will fix the app or fix the page.

Schools and districts

  • No data is collected, so there is nothing to sign a data-processing agreement about.
  • No data is collected from children, so COPPA, FERPA and UK GDPR obligations around pupil data do not attach to anything we hold. We hold nothing.
  • There is no paid tier, no in-app purchase and no advertising.
  • Source is public and licensed GPL-3.0-or-later, so it can be reviewed, forked and self-hosted.
